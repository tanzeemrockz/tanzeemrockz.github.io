Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how we deploy, maintain, and transfer your site.

What happens after the first year of hosting and domain?

Domain registration and static hosting renew at exact cost (typically ~₹1,200/year for `.in` or `.com` plus DNS). We never charge mandatory agency retainers.

Do I need to write text or provide finished designs?

No. Send us your product photos, catalog sheets, address, and a brief description of your services. We format the typography, write the copy, and assemble the page structure for you.

What is the payment milestone breakdown?

We charge a 50% advance (₹3,500) to secure your staging deployment slot and register your domain. The final 50% balance is payable only after you verify the working prototype on your phone.
